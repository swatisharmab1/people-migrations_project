<h2>🇮🇳 India - 🇵🇰 Pakistan Partition Migration, 1947</h2>

 

<p>

When British India was divided into India and Pakistan, huge numbers of people

moved across newly created borders. Many Hindus and Sikhs moved toward India,

while many Muslims moved toward Pakistan. This was one of the largest population

transfers in history.

</p>

 

<ul>

<li><b>Why did people move?</b> New borders, fear, safety and family connections.</li>

<li><b>How did they travel?</b> By train, foot, bullock carts, trucks and refugee convoys.</li>

<li><b>Impact:</b> Families, cities and cultures across South Asia were deeply changed.</li>

</ul>
